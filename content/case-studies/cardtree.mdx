Working on Cardtree as a Product and UX/UI Designer was a deeply enriching experience that blended creative design with strategic product thinking

**Cardtree is a digital identity platform that helps professionals and teams create dynamic, branded profiles that go beyond static link-in-bio tools**

By combining visual polish with usability, I aimed to maximize engagement and scalability across the product. This role allowed me to think beyond pixels, diving into problem discovery, user behavior, and feature prioritization ultimately helping shape a cohesive, flexible, and growth-ready design system for Cardtree
