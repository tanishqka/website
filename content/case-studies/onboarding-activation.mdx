## Re-imagining the Blank Canvas

The most intimidating moment for any creator is an empty viewport. Rather than confronting users with a blank canvas and a wall of text, we created the **Interactive Starter Sandbox**.

## Gamified Milestones

By introducing subtle micro-haptics, tactile stamps, and celebratory canvas particles upon completing initial pipeline steps, first-time users developed immediate muscle memory without feeling patronized.
